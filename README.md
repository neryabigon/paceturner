# PaceTurner

PaceTurner is a free RSVP (one-word-at-a-time) book reader for Garmin fēnix 8 watches.
This repository hosts the Android companion app and is the place to report problems.

## Install

1. Install **Garmin Connect Mobile** on your Android phone (Android 7 or newer) and pair your watch.
2. Download the latest `PaceTurner-<version>.apk` from [Releases](../../releases).
   Your phone will ask you to allow installing apps from your browser; allow it, then install.
3. Install **PaceTurner** on your watch from the Connect IQ Store.
4. Open the companion, import an EPUB, TXT or Markdown book, and send it to the watch.
   Then open PaceTurner on the watch.

Tested only on the fēnix 8 47 mm. Other fēnix 8 models are supported but untested.

## Support

Found a bug or have a question? [Open an issue](../../issues). Please include your watch
model and what you were doing.

## Privacy

PaceTurner collects no personal data. Your books, settings and reading positions stay on
your phone and watch.
